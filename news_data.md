# News facts for 24 November 2026 — checked 24 Nov 2026 (Reuters / CNN / SCMP / Al Jazeera)

All items below happened or were first reported on Tuesday 24 November 2026
unless a different date is stated in the item itself.

## Lead: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang
- Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday 23 November to restore Jensen
  Huang as chief executive, president and director.
- Huang resigned 11 days earlier (12 November) amid a scandal over an
  extramarital affair and alleged links to a suspected Chinese spy.
- The board said it had taken advice from senior US government officials and
  found no national security risk; its investigation continues.
- US President Donald Trump called the decision "the right and proper" one.
- Nvidia shares rose 30% in a single day, one of the biggest one-day gains
  in the company's history. Interim chief Colette Kress stays on as CFO.
- Huang is due to meet congressional committees behind closed doors on
  1 December and testify publicly to the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December.

## Kyiv strikes
- Russian air strikes on the Kyiv region killed at least 14 people, with
  several more missing, Ukrainian officials said.
- President Volodymyr Zelensky called for four-way talks to happen soon.

## US midterms aftermath
- Democrats won their largest House majority in decades in the midterm elections.
- US stocks rebounded on Monday, recovering the losses that followed the midterms.

## COP31 aftermath (Antalya — Sunday 22 November, NOT the lead)
- COP31 climate talks in Antalya collapsed on Sunday 22 November — the longest
  COP ever, running about 49 hours over time.
- The EU and more than 130 countries refused to sign a text with no
  fossil-fuel phase-out clause. The UN secretary-general warned of the failure;
  condemnation followed on Monday 23 November.
